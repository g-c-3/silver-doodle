Privacy Policy — Match Emojis Daily

Match Emojis Daily ("the App", "we", "us") is a daily match-3 puzzle game published by GC3 Studio Inc. ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, how it is used and stored, and the choices available to players. It applies to the Android app and any associated backend services (authentication, leaderboards, score validation, and analytics) described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information (provided by you)

To play, a player creates an account using email-based sign-in (a one-time confirmation link — no password is ever set or stored by us). We collect:

  • Email address — used solely to sign you in and, if you request it, to verify a change of email address. It is never shown to other players.
  • Display name — chosen by you, shown to other players on leaderboards. Do not use your real name if you'd prefer to stay pseudonymous; any name you choose is treated as public.

1.2 Gameplay data

To run daily puzzles, leaderboards, and prevent cheating, we record, per attempt:

  • Start and completion time, score, time bonus, lives used, and levels reached
  • Which daily puzzle definition was played and in what order
  • Whether an attempt was completed or abandoned (forfeited)

Gameplay moves themselves (the sequence of tile swaps you make) are sent to our server once, at the end of an attempt, purely to verify the score is legitimate. This move data is used only for that one-time verification and is not stored after verification completes — only the resulting summary (score, time bonus, etc. above) is kept.

1.3 Advertising data

The App shows optional, player-initiated rewarded video ads (e.g. to earn an extra life or unlock a bonus round) and, in some builds, banner or interstitial ads, served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its ad-serving partners may collect the advertising identifier, device information, and general location to select and measure ads. To verify that a rewarded ad was genuinely watched (server-side verification) before granting its in-game reward, the App also sends AdMob a per-player identifier tied to your account (specifically, an internal account ID, not your email address) alongside that verification request — this lets Google's server confirm the completed ad back to us for the correct player. We do not control what AdMob's ad partners themselves do with this data beyond what Google publishes in its own policies (linked in Section 5).

Your email address is only ever stored in our Supabase database and Supabase's own authentication system — it is never sent to AdMob or any other third party. The App does not currently use Firebase, Google Analytics, or any other analytics or crash-reporting service — the only third-party data flow beyond Supabase is the AdMob one described above. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in
  • To run the daily puzzle, track your progress, and show leaderboards
  • To verify that submitted scores are legitimate (server-side score replay) and to detect abandoned or forfeited attempts
  • To understand how the App is used, fix bugs, and improve it (analytics/crash reporting)
  • To show and verify rewarded/interstitial/banner ads, and to measure ad performance
  • To communicate with you about your account if you contact us (e.g. a support request)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your email address for marketing.

4. Your Choices and Rights

  • Display name — editable at any time from the in-app Profile screen.
  • Email address — changeable at any time from the in-app Profile screen, via a confirmation link sent to the new address.
  • Delete your account — the fastest way is in-app: Profile > Delete account. This is immediate and permanent. If you can't access the app, see this page for how to request deletion another way.
  • Access your data — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, contact us using the details in Section 10.
  • Advertising identifier — you can reset or limit ad tracking for your device from your Android device's own Settings > Privacy > Ads menu, which applies to AdMob the same as any other app using it.

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy governing how it separately handles data on its platform:

  • Supabase — supabase.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Children's Privacy

Match Emojis Daily does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age applicable in their jurisdiction (13 in most regions; higher in some). The App does not ask for or collect birthdate, age, phone number, or address at any point, and account creation only requires an email address and a display name. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take steps to remove it.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and gameplay summary data is kept for as long as your account exists, so that leaderboards, stats, and history remain accurate.
  • Raw gameplay move data used for one-time score verification is not retained after verification.
  • If you request account deletion, we will remove your account and associated gameplay records, other than data we're required to keep for legal or security purposes.

8. Security

Account sign-in uses one-time email links rather than stored passwords. Database access is protected with row-level security so that, other than public leaderboard information, a player's own data can only be read by that player and never written directly by any client — all score-affecting writes are validated and performed by server-side functions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
