Delete Your Account — Match Emojis Daily

This page describes how to permanently delete your Match Emojis Daily account and all associated data, as required by Google Play's account deletion policy.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (fastest)

If you can still sign in:

  1. Open Match Emojis Daily and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

This deletes your account immediately and permanently — there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion here, if you can't access the app

Email [FILL IN — the same contact address used in privacy-policy.html] from the email address your account is registered under, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll verify the request is coming from the account's own registered address and delete it — normally within a few business days.

If you're emailing from a different address than the one on your account, let us know your in-app display name so we can locate the right account.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, and does not just deactivate or hide:

  • Your account itself (email, display name)
  • Every attempt you've played — scores, dates, levels reached
  • Your daily, weekly, and all-time leaderboard stats
  • Your ad-reward verification history

This is permanent and cannot be reversed or recovered once completed — there's no "restore my account" option. If you're not sure, you can also just stop using the app; your data won't be deleted automatically, but nothing further will be collected.

What this doesn't affect

Deleting your account doesn't affect data Google or your device separately hold about you (e.g. AdMob's own advertising identifier records) — see the Privacy Policy for what's collected and by whom, and Google's own Google Account data controls for anything on Google's side.
