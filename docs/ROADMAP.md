    **Score integrity (`server/functions/score-replay/index.ts`):**
    - Bonus rounds previously had no move cap, no per-move time floor, and no placement/repeat/no-lives rules — a forged bonus round could out-score an entire honest run. Fixed: `MAX_MOVES_PER_LEVEL` cap, `MIN_MS_PER_MOVE` floor matching the client's real 420ms input-lock (verified against `attempt.js`, not assumed), and placement rules (bonus only directly after a completed regular slot at a multiple of 3, never first, never repeated, no lives claimable).
    - `elapsedMsAtEnd` had an upper bound but no lower one — `0` banked an entire level's time budget as time bonus, on every level. Fixed with a per-move floor and a per-claimed-life floor (a life can only be claimed if its full 60s timeout could plausibly have elapsed).
    - Attempt completion was two separate statements (a status read, then an unconditional update) — a race allowed double-counting stats via a concurrent resubmission. Fixed: the update itself now carries the status condition and checks the affected-row count, making the transition atomic.
    - A forfeited attempt had no time limit on late submission. Fixed: bounded to 45 minutes after `forfeit-stale-attempts` marks it (a judgment call, not from either report — easy to retune from real usage).
    - A missing `ad_verifications` row (AdMob's SSV callback hadn't landed yet) failed the whole attempt immediately. Fixed: one server-side 3s backoff and re-check before failing, resolving the common race within the same request.
